The performance of the target adapter is evaluated over three types of experiments.
In the first type, the KOS characteristics are varied, namely the degree of perforation in the folds, while keeping the PID controller identical across tests.
For the second group of experiments, the PID controller's tuning is varied, while maintaining consistent KOS characteristics.
Finally, the performance is tested for a crawling gait to observe the effect of the method in a highly restricted setting.

\subsection{Varying the KOS Perforation}\label{subsec:varying-kos}
For the first experiment, the PID controller is tuned to a reasonable performance on a robot with KOS perforations of a 1.5--0.5~\si{\mm} skip-to-cut ratio.
The values of the targets in the random sequence are integer values ranging between -3 and -25~degrees.
The KOS perforations used are 0.25–0.25~mm, 0.5–0.5~mm, 1.5–0.5~mm, and 2--0.5~mm, representing various stages of KOS degradation.
